## Becoming a maintainer

Maintainers are added for sustained, high-quality contributions to the specification or
for maintaining an independent implementation. Open an issue or reach out to an existing
maintainer. See the path to a steering committee in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).
